Proxy rendering: an instrument that parks a constant DC offset after its notes (VB3-II with the
user's Overdrive / Tube Feedback preset) can now be rendered and published as a proxy; the music
and its reverb are preserved unchanged. Report: `docs/PROXY_TAIL_POLICY_V2_2026-10-05.md`;
steering: `docs/PORTABLE_INSTRUMENTS_AND_PROXIES.md` §14.2 step 8, §15.2, §15.6; architecture:
`docs/CURRENT_ARCHITECTURE.md` "Tail-policy v2".

## Root cause

VB3-II leaves −0.605 (−4.4 dBFS) of constant DC on both channels after any note. The tail
detector judged the absolute per-block peak, so the decayed reverb on top of that offset never
counted as silence: the render ran into the 30 s cap (`TailLimitReached`) and nothing was
published. The user's 10 Hz low-cut insert hides the offset in live playback but the proxy is, by
design, rendered before the inserts.

## Changed behaviour

- **Tail-policy v2 (`kTailPolicyVersion` 2).** The detector tracks each channel's offset with a
  0.5 s one-pole estimate and judges the residual |x − dc| per channel (louder channel wins, no
  mid-sum); threshold −70 dBFS, window 1.0 s, cap 30 s unchanged. A constant offset no longer
  keeps the tail alive; a real reverb, including its bass and slow components, still has to decay.
  Nothing in the rendered audio is filtered, faded or normalized — only the judgement changed. The
  readiness verification settles and flushes on the same meter and hands the parked offset to the
  render.
- **A parked offset is not "audible output".** The plausibility check judges the residual as
  well: notes with no response, or an offset only, fail with `NoAudibleOutput`; the readiness
  stimulus never counts.
- **Proxy playback past the asset end continues at the asset's resting level** (zero for
  offset-free proxies) instead of stepping back to zero — measured through the user's 10 Hz insert:
  no step at EOF, past EOF, at a loop wrap or in an offline mixdown across the end.
- **Older proxies keep their status.** Generations rendered under tail-policy v1 (or without
  recorded policy versions) are comparable with v2 and stay `Current` when everything else
  matches — also on a machine without the Primary. Nothing is re-rendered automatically; a manual
  *Render now* publishes under a new generation name and keeps the old file.
- Diagnostics: `--stability-proxy-render-probe … [--tail-policy-v1] [--retain-failed]`,
  `--stability-proxy-playback-edges <project copy> <trackId> <outDir>`.

## Known — open defect from this task

- **Transport Start / Stop step with an offset-bearing proxy.** When the proxy is the source and
  the transport starts or stops at a position where the asset sits on its parked offset (lead-in,
  pauses, tail, past EOF), the proxy boundary steps 0 ↔ −0.605; through the user's 10 Hz low-cut
  this is a pulse of −23.6 … −25.7 dBFS post-strip at the row's fader (≈ −19 dBFS at unity),
  followed by about two seconds of filter settle at ≈ −40 dBFS. The Primary does not produce it
  (its offset persists while stopped). Not fixed in 1.1.15; documented in the report §6 and the
  backlog (`docs/PHASE_PLAN.md`). Not every proxy transition is click-free.
- Unchanged from 1.1.14: repeated re-renders of a non-deterministic instrument may accumulate
  sibling files (never deleted automatically); already-published silent generations from earlier
  versions are not detected at load; AmpliTube 4 teardown crash remains intermittent.
