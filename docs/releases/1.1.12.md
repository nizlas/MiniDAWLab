## Changed behaviour

- **Overlapping MIDI clips: the topmost clip wins.** On every instrument / MIDI track the clips
  form a layer stack (stored order; a new take or paste lands on top). The topmost clip owns its
  whole window — rests and empty clips included — and clips underneath are heard only where
  nothing covers them, exactly like audio events. Notes of a covered clip end at the cover and
  resume (new attack) where the clip becomes audible again; sustain, CC and pitch bend follow
  the audible clip, with the winning clip's state restated at every switch. One rule for all
  MIDI clips: older arrangements that relied on additive overlap now play the topmost clip only
  (the underlying clips and their data are untouched). Realtime playback, offline WAV / MP3
  mixdown and proxy rendering share one bake, so they select the same events. Selection is per
  source track: Upper, Lower and Pedal rows into one organ still sound together.
- **Proxies rendered before this rule are stale** (fingerprint schema 2 — clip order is render
  data now). Auto-mode destinations re-render; the existing stale / missing-Primary handling is
  unchanged. Nothing is marked Current with a different audible result.
- **Cycle recording of MIDI.** Record with Cycle on: one count-in, then every pass becomes its own
  take on top of the previous one — first pass from the record start to the right locator, full
  passes between the locators, the last pass up to Stop (a stop exactly on the loop point adds
  no empty take). Keys, sustain and the pitch wheel held across the loop point carry over (note
  ends in one take, continues at the start of the next; controller state restated). A silent or
  controller-only pass is a take too and masks earlier material like an audio pass. Pass
  boundaries come from the engine's own wrap positions on a monotone clock (late-delivered MIDI
  across a wrap lands in the right pass; several wraps between UI updates are fine). Audio +
  MIDI cycle takes share the pass boundaries; the whole run is ONE undo step; nothing old is
  overwritten. Instrument tracks and MIDI tracks alike; MIDI-only or together with an audio take.
- **No doubling while recording.** A recording row's earlier takes are silent during the take
  (Record decides this, Monitor still decides whether you hear yourself); other rows, including
  other MIDI rows into the same instrument, keep playing.
- **Take preview fixed.** The red REC region starts exactly at the take's start and ends at the
  playhead (same position and transform as the playhead line); after a wrap it restarts at the
  left locator with earlier passes dimmed; zoom, scroll and Follow keep it anchored; count-in
  shows nothing. The old 40 px minimum made it start before the boundary and run ahead of the
  playhead.
- Recording at or past the end of the arrangement grows the arrangement so the transport keeps
  running through the take (30 min headroom).

  project reload.
