Follow-up to 1.1.8: the Inspector meter shows only the selected row's output, and the organ's
residual signal / mute click is isolated to its cause. Project-file schema unchanged (23). ASIO,
BPM transfer (1.1.4), export fixes (1.1.5), insert persistence / header column (1.1.7) and the
level report (1.1.8) untouched. Full report: `docs/ORGAN_DC_INSPECTOR_METER_2026-10-03.md`.

## Changed

- **Inspector channel panel shows one meter: the selected row's own audio output.** Audio /
  instrument / group row: that row's fader + its output meter. Stereo Out row: the master fader +
  the Stereo Out meter. MIDI row or no selected row: the panel is hidden and the Inspector content
  gets the full height. The extra always-visible Stereo Out meter from 1.1.8 is removed (this
  replaces the earlier "constant master display"). Export level report and diagnostics still meter
  the master internally.
- The meter's "DC" tag disappears when the row stops producing blocks (it used to stick on a muted
  row).

## Fixed

- **Muted instrument rows are now processed with gain 0 instead of being skipped.** Before, a muted
  (or −∞ fader) instrument row was skipped before its plug-in ran: MIDI routed to it from MIDI
  tracks piled up in the host's per-block buffer for the whole mute (audio-thread growth) and was
  delivered as one stale burst on unmute, the note-offs sent at mute were never processed (notes
  frozen in the plug-in), and the plug-in's state resumed mid-stream on unmute. Realtime, offline
  export and the shared strip helper now all treat mute / −∞ as a gain decision after the host has
  processed the block. Only Off skips the plug-in.

## Investigation result (organ residual level and mute click)

- The ~−10 dBFS reading on the VB3-II row with nothing playing is **real and almost pure DC**
  (+0.34 at the row, +0.19 at Stereo Out, AC component −71 dBFS), present in the current samples.
- Fresh-instance isolation, one parameter at a time: the DC is produced by the preset's
  **Rotary FX Tube Feedback (0.61; threshold between 0.25 and 0.5) + Overdrive 1.0 + amp model 2**.
  Reverting Tube Feedback to 0 or Overdrive to its default removes it; both organ-engine versions
  show it. It is the plug-in's DSP behaviour, not DAL's hosting.
- The click on mute / unmute with the transport stopped is that DC step being switched. It remains
  while the preset carries DC; no mute ramp, DC filter, EQ or normalisation was added.
- Reproduction package for an independent host (Cubase): `docs/evidence/organ-dc-2026-10-03/VB3-II-track5.vstpreset`
  + steps in the report. The Cubase comparison itself was not performed.

## Diagnostics

- `--stability-organ-dc <project>`: DC / AC measurement of the selected row and Stereo Out in
  stopped / muted / after-playback windows, host-processing check while muted, MIDI-per-block
  maxima across 4 s and 300 ms mutes while playing.
- `ExportLevelFocusedTests --probe-vst3-isolate <bundle> <project> <trackId> <outDir>`: fresh-instance
  parameter isolation; writes a `.vstpreset` of the track's saved state.
