# Danielssons Audio Lab 1.1.13

| | |
|---|---|
| Version | 1.1.13 |
| Date | 2026-10-05 |
| Installer | `DanielssonsAudioLab-1.1.13-Setup.exe` |

Corrections after 1.1.12 (commit `0b23421`): Inspector without the MIDI event counter, live
rendering while recording into a proxy-backed instrument, one stop boundary for audio and MIDI,
arrangement extent that grows on demand, and a precise rule for proxies rendered before the
layering change. Report: `docs/LIVE_MIDI_RECORDING_2026-10-03.md`, section "1.1.13 — corrections
after 1.1.12"; architecture: `docs/CURRENT_ARCHITECTURE.md` (live-MIDI bullets "One record run,
one stop boundary", "Arrangement extent grows on demand", "Primary / proxy / Secondary while
monitoring or recording", "Proxies"); steering: `docs/PORTABLE_INSTRUMENTS_AND_PROXIES.md` §11.4
and §17.

## Changed behaviour

- **Inspector: no event counter.** "MIDI received: N events" / "Ready — no MIDI received yet" are
  gone. A working input shows nothing and the status area collapses; incoming MIDI is the
  header's activity dot. Real obstacles are still shown (no MIDI Input, device not connected,
  device could not be opened, All MIDI inputs without a device, no destination, overflow).
- **Recording into an instrument that plays a proxy.** A MIDI take on a row into such a
  destination (the instrument row itself or a MIDI row routed to it) now switches the whole
  destination to its **Secondary** instrument for the duration of the take when one is
  configured and loadable — for the earlier clips *and* the live input, one consistent source.
  The recording row's own earlier takes are silent there (no doubling), other rows into the
  same instrument keep sounding, Monitor still decides whether you hear yourself. Arming alone
  changes nothing; the switch begins with the count-in and ends with Stop / abort, after which
  the proxy's currency is decided for real (a committed take makes it stale honestly; an undone
  one leaves it Current). Without a Secondary the proxy keeps playing as backing, the take is
  still recorded and the Inspector says "Recording: earlier material on this track is still
  heard from the proxy - no live instrument is available".
- **Audio and MIDI stop at the same sample.** The audio engine acknowledges the start and the
  stop of a recording run at a block boundary and both the audio take and the MIDI take are cut
  there (1.1.12 could differ by one device block). Cycle pass boundaries, the pass count, the
  last partial take and "no empty take on a stop exactly at the loop point" are unchanged; the
  separate audio / MIDI placement compensations are kept. A device that stops or disappears
  during a take no longer makes Stop wait: the take is finalized from a fallback position and
  the recorder is detached before anything is freed.
- **Arrangement grows while you record.** The 30-minute reserve at record start is gone. The
  transport keeps running past the previous end for audio-only, MIDI-only and combined takes
  (engine-side, independent of the UI), the navigation area follows at 10 Hz with a small
  margin, and at Stop the arrangement is the previous extent plus what the recording needs —
  nothing more. Cycle runs do not lengthen the project; saved extents are never shrunk;
  locators and export range are untouched.
- **Proxies rendered before 1.1.12.** A proxy rendered under the old additive rule stays
  **Current without a re-render** when its content is provably unaffected by the layering rule
  (no overlapping clips per track, stored order = playback order, every controller point inside
  its clip); every other such proxy is stale — never played, re-rendered automatically only in
  Auto mode on a machine where the Primary instrument is present (otherwise the Secondary or
  silence, with the status saying so). Proxy files, Primary identity and plugin state are
  untouched; there is no option for the old additive playback. This also fixes a defect found
  while verifying: re-rendering such a destination with the Primary present ended with "identity
  changed during finalizing — result discarded" and the proxy stayed stale forever.

## Known, unchanged or observed

- With Monitor off and **no Secondary**, a proxy destination keeps rendering the recording row's
  earlier takes from the WAV during a take (a shared proxy cannot mute one row) — reported in the
  Inspector.
- A plug-in that restores its state asynchronously (Groove Agent SE observed) reports a different
  live state revision for ~1–2 s after load, during which its destination reads Stale; it corrects
  itself once the state is restored.
- The same destination content rendered by the Debug and the Release build produced different
  proxy lengths (49.3 s vs 39.0 s — tail detection); the asset store then refuses to overwrite a
  same-named generation file of a different length ("generation-name collision"). Seen only in
  the test setup (sibling test copy sharing `InstrumentProxies`); not investigated further.
- AmpliTube 4 teardown crash (`AmpliTube 4.vpa+0x7675E`) remains intermittent at shutdown.
