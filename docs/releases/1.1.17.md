Mixer window refinements (UI only). Report: `docs/MIXER_WINDOW_2026-10-06.md` §5; architecture:
`docs/CURRENT_ARCHITECTURE.md` "Mixer window".

## Changed

- **Pan** in the mixer is the Inspector's pan control at its full size: the stick above the
  readout, drag, Ctrl-click to centre, double-click to type, the blue fill from the centre — on
  every channel, also on channels that are not selected.
- **Colours** follow the rest of DAL: channel headers use the arrangement header's colours and
  active marking, the routing / pre-gain / insert / send sections sit on the Inspector's lighter
  grey, the fader / meter area keeps its darker plate. Pre insert rows are blue, Post insert rows
  orange (before / after the fader); an unavailable plug-in keeps its "(unavailable)" text with a
  red outline.
- **Draggable section dividers**: grey lines between the sections run across the whole mixer
  including Stereo Out; drag one (↕ cursor) to change the height split between the two adjacent
  sections for all channels at once. Routing, pre-gain and sends never shrink below their
  controls, insert lists scroll when their band is low, faders keep their usable height. Hidden
  sections leave no divider and come back at their stored height; a small window scrolls.
- **All inserts are reachable**: each Pre / Post chain is a scrolling list in the strip — the band
  height decides how many rows are visible, more by scrolling or by dragging the band taller;
  "+ Add" and the section title stay put. Click opens the editor; right-click offers move up /
  down, move to the other stage, remove.
- Section heights are remembered per machine in `ui-layout.xml` (one write per completed drag),
  next to the mixer bounds, section flags and track-header width.

## Verified

- `MixerFocusedTests` 107 checks (height clamps, dividers, drag redistribution, pan drag on a
  non-active strip, six-insert list with slot-bound actions, store round trip).
- `--stability-mixer` on a copy of the TSE project (Debug and Release): pan drag → row and
  Inspector agree, divider drags with aligned strips, six Post inserts listed / scrolled /
  removed by slot, heights persisted and kept in a 500 px window, meters unchanged.
- `TrackHeaderColumnFocusedTests` 149, `InputRoutingFocusedTests` 56 — 0 failures (shared
  header plate / glyphs).

## Known

- No drag-and-drop reordering of inserts in the mixer (context menu); no solo; no fader undo —
  unchanged model. Real pointer input was not automated (handler-level gestures in the tests).
